== Preface

[.quote-box]
____
You are already enough.
____

Welcome to a journey toward understanding this simple truth — not just in your head, but in your heart.

The Centering workshop is a five-session series grounded in DBT (Dialectical Behavior Therapy) and Viktor Frankl's existential psychology. It is designed to help you discover your unconditional self-worth and find your inner center.

We often try to prove our value through achievement, the approval of others, or the pursuit of perfection. But this workshop asks a different question: _What happens when you discover that you are already enough?_

'''

=== How This Book Is Organized

This booklet is structured as five sessions:

1. *Noticing Your Inner Voice* — Inner talk and automatic response patterns
2. *The Iceberg Model and Separating Observation from Interpretation* — Non-judgmental self-observation
3. *Unconditional Self-Worth* — A value that needs no proof
4. *Fullness Flows* — Giving and circulation
5. *Maturity — A Lifelong Question* — The journey of a lifetime

Each session is organized as follows:

- *Episode*: A relatable, real-world story
- *Core Concepts*: Theoretical background and explanation
- *Practice Exercises*: Methods you can apply in daily life

[TIP]
====
This book is best read in order, but if a particular topic speaks to where you are right now, feel free to start there.
====

=== How to Read This Book

==== Slowly, with Space

This is not a book to race through. As you read each session's story, let it bring your own experiences to mind. *Living the experience* matters more than merely understanding the concepts.

==== Read While Practicing

Do not skip the practice exercises at the end of each session. Actually doing the exercises is an entirely different experience from simply reading about them.

==== Be Compassionate with Yourself

As you read, you may notice shortcomings or mistakes in yourself. Please do not criticize yourself for them. Awareness itself is already significant growth.

'''

=== Theoretical Background

This workshop draws on the following psychological theories and approaches:

- *DBT (Dialectical Behavior Therapy)* — Marsha Linehan
- *NVC (Nonviolent Communication)* — Marshall Rosenberg
- *Existential Psychology* — Viktor Frankl
- *Mindfulness* — Jon Kabat-Zinn
- *Humanistic Psychology* — Carl Rogers, Abraham Maslow
