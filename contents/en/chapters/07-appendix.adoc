=== Practice Sheets

The following practice sheets can be used in daily life. Feel free to copy or adapt them.

==== Inner Voice Observation Journal

[.practice-box]
====
*Date:*

*Situation:* What happened?

*Inner voice:* What thoughts came up?

*Physical sensations:* What did you feel in your body?

*What you noticed:* Did you spot any patterns in this voice?
====

==== Separating Observation from Interpretation

[.practice-box]
====
*Situation:*

*Observable facts:* (without judgment)

*My interpretation:* (how I received it)

*Other possible interpretations:* (could I see it differently?)

*Real emotions and needs:* (what is truly felt, beneath the interpretation)
====
